Projection assumptions

IR, suspended, DNR and taxi players are excluded. An Out designation excludes a player for the current week only; later weeks assume they can return. Questionable players are assumed to play. Bye weeks and your league’s starting positions are included.

These are early estimates, not guarantees. Missing player data, roster changes and future injuries can change the outlook.
