Projection assumptions

IR, suspended, DNR and taxi players are excluded. An Out designation excludes a player for the current week only; later weeks assume they can return. Questionable players are assumed to play. Bye weeks and your league’s starting positions are included.

These are early estimates, not guarantees. Short game histories use their available results with wider scoring ranges. Uncovered starting spots add no points; future waiver additions are not assumed. Current-week percentages are full-game estimates, not live odds. Records are rounded from 5,000 simulated seasons while preserving the season total and completed results for each team. Win and finish ranges contain the middle 80% of simulated outcomes. Playoff odds use the unrounded simulations and default record, points-for and higher-points-against rules, including supported division qualifiers. Custom qualification rules and commissioner seed overrides are not included. The opening round is a projected field, not a guaranteed bracket; later-round reseeding and championship outcomes are not included yet. Future roster moves, injuries and NFL teammate correlations are not modeled.
