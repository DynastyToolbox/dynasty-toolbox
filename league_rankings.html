League Roster Rankings

We use three ranking systems on this page: Overall is our best reflection of true dynasty value, while Contending and Rebuilding are more “value-to-you” systems Rebuilding if you’re chasing a title today, Rebuilding if you’re rebuilding for the future.

Where to find you Sleeper league ID:

Mobile: Located at the bottom of "General Settings" for your league

Web Browser: Located in your league url (sleeper.com/leagues/1000000000000000001/)

How picks and team scores work

Team scores average the top 15 player and pick values. PICK ranks average the best 10 pick values. Dynasty picks cover the next three undrafted seasons at published round values; Unranked picks count as zero. Originally identifies the current manager of the original team. Future projections use player values plus the existing rank-based adjustments; future picks are not directly added to those projected values.
