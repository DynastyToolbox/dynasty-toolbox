Mock Draft Simulator

This tool generates a dynasty mock draft based on where players are selected. Tap the plus icon next to any available player, then click an empty cell in the draft board to assign them. When you’re finished, click Finish Draft to create your mock.

Players receive position specific scores based on draft round, hit rates, and team opportunity. All players at the same position share the same base score, so any WR taken in the same round and landing spot will receive the same value.

Enable Include Devy Rankings to use unique base scores for each player, derived from Dynasty Toolbox’s devy rankings.
