How matches are found

We compare the combined value of each team’s top three ranked players at QB, RB, WR and TE with the league median for that position, using that team’s chosen ranking system and the scoring filters. A position more than 20% above the median is depth; more than 20% below is a need. Picks and unranked players do not determine positional needs.

Trade ideas include player swaps, players for picks and packages: 1-for-1, 2-for-1, 3-for-1 and 2-for-2, in either direction. Each side has at most three assets and two picks. When more assets go one way, the main piece must be worth at least half the best asset coming back. Unranked assets are left out.

The gap between the two sides’ unweighted Overall values must be no more than 15% of the larger total. Each team must also come out no worse than −1,000 under its own rankings and pick preferences, including the calculator’s existing Stud Bonus. Team needs and gains under both teams’ preferences help order the ideas. The Overall market check never uses pick preferences or Stud Bonus.

We show up to six ideas per partner, with a mix of main players and picks. For unusually deep rosters, the search uses the top 40 ranked players and 24 ranked picks by Overall value. These are possible starting points for a conversation, not predictions that another manager will accept.
