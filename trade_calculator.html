How league ranks and picks work

Contending and rebuilding scores average each roster's best 15 player and pick values, using the scoring filters above. Ranks compare all teams in the league before and after every transfer. Ties are ordered by roster ID. The trade's stud bonus does not affect roster ranks. Unranked assets count as zero. Picks cover the next three undrafted seasons, with the original team's current manager shown. League data is cached for up to one minute; refresh clears the trade and reloads rosters and picks.
